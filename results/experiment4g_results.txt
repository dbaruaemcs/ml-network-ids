INTERPRETATION
======================================================================

The Kolmogorov-Smirnov statistic measures the difference between the empirical distributions of two attack families. Higher values indicate greater distribution difference.

Large distribution differences can indicate feature-level domain shift and may help explain poor cross-attack generalization.
